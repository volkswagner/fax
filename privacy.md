# Privacy Policy for Fax

_Last Updated: 5/27/2025

Thank you for using **Fax**, a custom app available through the Frappe Cloud Marketplace.

We take your privacy seriously. This policy outlines what data we collect and how it is used.

## 1. Information Collection

We do **not** collect, store, or transmit any personal information, usage data, or metrics through the **Fax** app. The app runs entirely within your Frappe/ERPNext environment and does not send any data externally.

## 2. Third-Party Services

While **Fax** itself does not collect any data, it may be distributed or hosted using third-party platforms, such as:

- **Frappe Cloud** – Please refer to the [Frappe Cloud Privacy Policy](https://frappecloud.com/privacy) for details on how they handle data.
- **GitHub** – The source code for **Fax** may be hosted or downloaded from GitHub. Refer to the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement) for information on GitHub’s data handling practices.

## 3. Updates to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. Continued use of the app constitutes your acceptance of any changes.

If you have any questions or concerns, please refer to the contact information on the Frappe Cloud Marketplace listing or the GitHub repository.
